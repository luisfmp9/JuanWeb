COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a price quote?

Send a brief description and photos for smaller jobs, or schedule an on-site walkthrough for larger projects so we can give you an accurate estimate.

What are your rates and minimum charge?

Our minimum per job is $270.00. Final pricing depends on the scope of work, project time, and materials needed. If you have a punch list of smaller repairs, we can group them together in one visit.

Do you offer a warranty on your work?

Yes. All repairs and installations are backed by a 1-year workmanship warranty. If an issue comes up with our labor within 12 months, we fix it at no labor cost. For materials we supply, we also handle store warranty exchanges on defective products (customer-supplied materials remain under manufacturer warranty; normal wear and tear or accidental damage are excluded).

What are your payment terms?

We require 70% upfront before starting the job to lock in your project dates and cover materials, and 30% upon completion once the work is finished.

Do you handle rental turnover cleaning and repairs between tenants?

Yes. When tenants move out, we repair wall, floor, or countertop damage, complete deep or move-out cleaning, and prepare the unit for the next residents.

How long do repairs take?

Many smaller repairs take 1 to 4 hours, while larger installations, painting, or multi-coat finishes like deck staining can take longer depending on prep and drying times.

What does drywall repair include?

Cutting out damaged areas, taping and applying joint compound, sanding smooth, matching texture, and priming or painting as needed.

Why do doors stick?

Settling, seasonal humidity shifts, or worn hinges and misaligned strike plates often cause sticking; adjusting hinges, shimming frames, or planing edges usually resolves it.

When should I repair fences and prep gutters or decks in Colorado?

Clearing gutters and fixing fences before winter helps prevent freeze-thaw and water damage, while spring is ideal for spotting winter wear. Decks in Colorado generally need resealing every 2 to 3 years due to strong sun and dry air.
