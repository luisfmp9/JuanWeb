Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026 • Juan Narro Masonry & Construction (juannarro.com)

1. Information We Collect

When you request an estimate or contact Juan Narro through juannarro.com, we collect only the personal details you voluntarily provide, including your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, and project description.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • Preparing accurate masonry, concrete, or construction labor and material estimates.
  • Scheduling on-site inspections or virtual consultations.
  • Communicating project updates, invoices, and service confirmations.

3. Data Sharing & Protection

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers. Information is shared only when strictly necessary with trusted material suppliers or permit authorities with your prior consent.

This website operates as a lightweight static platform and does not use invasive behavioral tracking cookies. Basic server logs may record anonymous technical data (browser type, referring page) to maintain site security and uptime.
