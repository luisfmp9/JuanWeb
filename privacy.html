Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Juan Narro Masonry & Construction (juannarro.com)

1. Scope & Overview

Juan Narro ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share when visiting juannarro.com or requesting construction and masonry estimates in the United States.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily submit through our quote request form or direct communications, which may include:

  • Contact Details: Full name, phone number (for calls or SMS project follow-ups), and email address.
  • Project Location & Scope: Property ZIP code, service category, approximate dimensions, and repair or remodeling descriptions.
  • Technical Logs: Standard non-identifying server logs (browser type, device category, and referring URL) generated automatically by our hosting infrastructure to ensure security and uptime.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Evaluate project feasibility and prepare labor and material estimates.
  • Schedule on-site walkthroughs, measurements, or virtual consultations.
  • Send service confirmations, written proposals, and project updates.

4. Third-Party Service Providers & No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. To deliver form submissions securely from our website to our inbox, we use third-party form processing infrastructure (such as Web3Forms), which processes your inquiry solely on our behalf. Information may only be shared with material suppliers or municipal permitting offices when strictly required to fulfill your contracted project and with your prior authorization.

5. U.S. State Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA & State Laws)

Depending on your state of residence in the United States, you may have the right to request disclosure of the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or ask for the deletion of your contact records. To exercise any of these rights, contact us directly using the details below.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

This website and our construction services are intended strictly for adults (18 years and older) and property owners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Data Retention & Contact Information

Inquiry records are retained only as long as necessary to provide estimates, fulfill contracted work, or comply with tax and business record-keeping laws. For privacy inquiries or deletion requests, contact Juan Narro at contact@juannarro.com or call +1 (555) 234-8910.
